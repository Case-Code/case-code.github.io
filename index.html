How we work

Online is the method, not a compromise.

We have been a distributed company since June 2020. No office means no overhead priced into your quote, no time lost to a commute, and no geography limiting who we hire or who we work for. What replaces the office is discipline: everything in version control, everything written down, and a build you can run yourself from day one.

Scope

We agree what “done” means

Before any code: the problem, the constraints, the platforms, and a fixed definition of the first release. Written down, so nobody is guessing later.

Build

You watch it happen

Work lands in small reviewable pieces on a branch you have access to. You see progress in commits and builds, not in status meetings.

Hand over

You keep the keys

The repository, the pipeline and the documentation are yours at the end. Stay on a retainer if you want us; leave cleanly if you don’t.
